Privacy policy

Updated 29 September 2026 · Jelly Merge for Android and iOS

Jelly Merge is provided by Eugen-Andrei Rad, publishing as Andrei Rad, in Romania. For privacy questions or requests, email andrei@age-one.com.

Your game stays on your device

Jelly Merge stores game progress, scores, discoveries, settings, cosmetic unlocks and saved rounds locally. Saved rounds include the board and the information needed to continue playing. We do not operate game accounts, cloud saves, online leaderboards or a game-data backend. An optional one-time Remove Ads purchase removes advertising and unlocks the Starlight cosmetic.

The app does not send us your saved board or scores. Your operating system may include local app data in device backups according to its settings. Clearing app data or removing the app can remove this progress; operating-system backups are controlled separately.

Age choice and optional videos

The app may ask for an age band before offering advertising. It stores only the selected band locally, not a date of birth. You can choose not to answer. For anyone under 18, anyone who prefers not to say and anyone who has not answered, the app does not contact Google advertising or consent services.

If you select 18 or older and choose to use the optional rewarded-video feature, the app uses Google AdMob and Google's consent service to manage choices, request and show a video where available. Starlight is a cosmetic reward and is also available through gameplay. Refusing an optional video does not block playing the game.

Information used by Google's services

When those services are used, Google may process your IP address and approximate location derived from it; ad and app interactions such as taps and video views; diagnostic and performance information; and device or advertising identifiers, subject to platform permissions and your choices. This information supports ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising partners may receive it.

Non-personalized ads can still use data and identifiers for purposes such as measurement and fraud prevention. They do not mean that no information leaves your device. Read the Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information for its processing, retention and controls.

Where consent is required, the app asks through Google's consent form before requesting ads. Privacy choices are accessible from Skins or Settings when required or available for your region and consent state. Your device also provides advertising privacy controls. Withdrawing consent affects future processing; it does not undo processing that has already occurred.

Optional purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play processes payments using your store account. Jelly Merge does not receive your card number or payment details and does not send purchase receipts to a developer-operated server. The app uses native store purchase records to verify and restore the permanent Remove Ads entitlement, and stores its entitlement locally so it works offline. No Unity Analytics, Unity Authentication or external checkout is used for this purchase.

Restore purchases is available in the app for the same store account. Purchases do not transfer between Apple and Google accounts. Store purchase records, parental purchase controls and refund requests are managed by the relevant store under the Apple Privacy Policy or Google Privacy Policy. An active Remove Ads entitlement prevents new advertising requests; it does not erase information previously processed by an advertising provider.

If you contact support

We receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer you, investigate the reported issue and keep necessary support correspondence. This relies on our legitimate interest in supporting the game and responding to requests. Email delivery and storage involve the email providers used by you and the developer. The app does not send messages or diagnostic attachments automatically.

This website

This site has no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded social widgets or cookies set by us. Images and fonts are hosted with the site. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to that provider's service and privacy practices.

Keeping and deleting information

Local progress remains on your device until it is replaced or removed. We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations, then delete information no longer needed. Google, GitHub and email providers manage their own retention and may process information in countries outside yours, including outside the European Economic Area, under the safeguards described in their policies.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information we hold, or object to processing. Contact the email above. We cannot access or delete local game data on your behalf, and provider-held data may require a request to that provider. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority, including Romania's ANSPDCP.

Children and changes to this policy

We do not ask children to provide personal details or use advertising or consent services for age bands below 18. Parents or guardians can contact us if a child has sent personal information by email so we can address it. If the game's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date and any additional notice or consent required.
