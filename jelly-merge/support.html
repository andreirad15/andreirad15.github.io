How do I drop a jelly?

Move your finger to aim, then tap to drop. Hold or swipe down for a faster drop. Match jellies of the same tier and keep the pile below the danger line. The Pause menu also contains the controls.

Can I continue my round later?

Use the menu's Continue action to return to a saved round. Normal and Daily runs have separate local saves. A Daily save is available on its original UTC day. Restart starts a new round and replaces that mode's saved run.

Where is my progress stored?

Scores, discoveries, cosmetics, settings and saved rounds are stored on your device. There is no Jelly Merge account or cloud sync. Removing the app or clearing its data can remove progress. Device backups are managed by your operating system, and support cannot restore a server copy that does not exist.

How does the Daily Challenge work?

A new challenge follows the UTC date. The same day's retries use the same incoming jelly sequence and rule. Daily records stay separate from Normal records and from different Daily rules. Scores are local; there is no online leaderboard.

Do I need to watch a video?

No. The game and its progression remain playable without ads. Players who choose the 18-or-older age band may opt into a rewarded video for the Starlight cosmetic when a video is available. Starlight can also be unlocked through gameplay. A video does not grant score, power upgrades or better shiny odds.

Where can I change ad privacy choices?

Open Privacy choices in Skins or Settings when it is available for your region and consent state. You can also use your device's advertising privacy controls. Players under 18, those who prefer not to answer and players who have not answered the age question do not use the ad or consent services.

Email opens your own mail app. Jelly Merge does not automatically send a support report or attach your saved game. See the privacy policy for how support messages are handled.
