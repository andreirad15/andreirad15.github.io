Light every bulb.

Circuits is a calm puzzle game for Android and iOS. Draw wires across the board, connect the battery to every bulb and press TEST. 100 handmade levels in 10 chapters add resistors, switches, bridges, diodes, relays, fuses and multi-section boards.

In preparation

The game is not yet available in the stores.

No timer

Undo, restart and test as often as you like. Hints show the next stretch of wire.

Works offline

No account needed. Progress is saved on your device.

Fair ads

Ads are never needed to solve a level. Remove Ads is an optional purchase.
