Local game data

The game does not require an account. Level progress, solved circuits, hints, settings and records needed to avoid duplicate rewards or purchases are saved on your device. We do not operate a cloud-save, multiplayer, chat or leaderboard service for this release.

The puzzles work offline. Your operating system or a device backup may copy app data according to its own settings; that is separate from a publisher cloud save. Advertising, privacy-message and store services can process data off the device as described below.

Audience

Circuits is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not ask for or store a date of birth. Parents and guardians can contact us with questions or requests about their child's information. Please avoid sending unnecessary information about a child.

Advertising and privacy messages

The game uses Unity LevelPlay/ironSource to mediate advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for regional privacy messages. Ads are shown as a banner on menu and result screens, an occasional interstitial between levels, and optional reward videos that you choose to watch for a hint. Ads are never required to solve a level; unavailable ads do not block play.

Advertising services can process IP address and approximate location derived from it, device/app identifiers (such as the advertising ID), ad views and interactions, app interactions, crash logs and performance information. Uses can include delivering and measuring ads, advertising analytics, preventing fraud and diagnosing SDK problems. Device identifiers or ad interactions may be shared among mediation and advertising partners; this can constitute tracking or targeted advertising depending on the provider, settings, choices and applicable law. A lack of a game account does not make advertising data anonymous.

UMP contacts Google when refreshing your privacy status or presenting required choices, and stores consent records so your choices can be applied. We do not treat silence as consent; the game checks the available regional privacy choices before requesting ads. See Google's UMP information.

Where privacy choices apply, open Settings → Ad privacy choices to review or change them. Declining personalized advertising may still permit limited or non-personalized ads under the provider's settings and applicable requirements. On iOS, Apple tracking permission is a separate choice if tracking is requested; refusing it does not prevent play. These controls affect future processing and do not automatically delete previous records.

For provider processing, retention, international transfers and request routes, read Unity's player privacy policy and Google's privacy policy. Advertising partners' use of identifiers may fall within legal definitions of selling or sharing personal information; use the available privacy choices and contact us about your applicable rights.

Purchases

An optional Remove Ads purchase removes banner and interstitial ads permanently in this game; optional reward videos remain available. Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never receive your card or bank details. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to talk to the store. The store and Unity can process purchase details, transaction identifiers, device information and diagnostics to complete, verify and restore the purchase. The game stores on your device which products you own and the transaction identifiers needed to avoid granting a purchase twice. See Google, Apple and Unity privacy information.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer the request and keep an appropriate record of our response. Please do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal details. Our email provider also processes the correspondence.

This static website has no analytics code, advertisements, forms, embedded media, custom cookies or remote fonts. The host, GitHub Pages, may process request information such as IP address and browser/network details to deliver and secure the site. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Use, sharing and retention

We use local data to provide the game and support information to respond to you. Where European data-protection law applies, the basis depends on the activity: providing a service you request, consent where required, legitimate interests in support and security, and legal obligations. Provider purposes and legal bases are explained in their own notices.

Local data stays until it is replaced during play, app storage is cleared or the app is deleted, subject to operating-system backups. Support correspondence is kept while needed to handle the request and related follow-up, and longer only where legally necessary. Advertising and store providers keep their own records under their own notices.

Information may be processed by hosting, email, advertising, store and technical-service providers outside your country, including outside the EEA, under the safeguards those providers apply. Information can also be disclosed when required by law. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Choices and data requests

  • Local data: Android app-storage controls can clear the game's data. On iOS, deleting the app removes its local data. Clearing data erases your progress; a Remove Ads purchase can be restored from the store.
  • Advertising: use Settings → Ad privacy choices where shown, and your device's advertising and tracking settings.
  • Requests: email rovideotuts@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion, or to ask about another privacy right. Include the platform and app version if known.

Where applicable, rights also include portability, restriction, objection and withdrawal of consent. We respond as required by applicable law, generally within one month for GDPR requests. You may complain to your local supervisory authority, including Romania's ANSPDCP.

Changes and contact

We update this page when release behavior, providers or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the current revision. Contact Eugen Andrei Rad, Romania, at rovideotuts@gmail.com.
