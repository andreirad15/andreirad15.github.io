How to play

Drag from a terminal or wire to draw a path. Every bulb must sit in a closed loop with the battery, without a short circuit. Press TEST to send current through the board. Pinch or use two fingers to zoom and pan on large boards; Fit shows the whole board again.

Hints

You start with free hints and earn one when you finish a chapter. A hint shows the next few cells of the solution. When you run out, you can choose to watch a reward video for one more hint.

Ads

A banner can appear on menu and result screens, and an occasional ad between levels, never during a puzzle. Reward videos are always optional. If an ad is not available, the game simply continues. Privacy choices are under Settings → Ad privacy choices where they apply.

Remove Ads and restore

Remove Ads removes banner and interstitial ads permanently in this game. Optional reward videos remain available. After reinstalling or changing device, open the shop and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same store account. Refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store.

Progress and data

Progress is saved on your device only. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling deletes it. See the privacy policy for details.
