Who we are

This policy covers the Glidori mobile game and these official information and support pages. The publisher is EUGEN ANDREI RAD. You can contact us at andrei.rad@syncorm.com.

You can play without creating a Glidori account. The game has no player chat, user uploads or app-managed cloud saves.

What the game remembers locally

The game stores your coin wallet, unlocked and selected vehicles, best score, finish goals, selected finish and any unused one-run finish reward on your device. It also remembers sound and haptic settings, control order, the age range you choose and a local cache of verified Remove Ads ownership.

These records keep your choices between sessions. The game does not send your wallet balance, vehicle collection or control preferences to a publisher-operated game server. Removing the app or clearing its data can remove local progress. Your operating system’s backup settings may preserve or restore some data.

Coin-unlocked vehicles and earned finishes are local game progress. They are separate from a store purchase, so Restore Purchases does not recover that progress.

Advertising and provider data

Glidori uses Google AdMob for banners, optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between runs. We request non-personalized ads and set the provider’s maximum ad-content rating to G. Non-personalized advertising still processes data for ad delivery, reporting, security and fraud prevention.

Google’s advertising services may process your IP address and approximate location, app context, permitted device or account identifiers, ad views and interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics. The data and uses depend on platform, age protections and privacy choices. These services can perform advertising measurement and analytics even though the game does not include a separate gameplay-analytics service.

The game disables the advertising SDK’s publisher first-party ID setting and does not initialize advertising mediation. Age protections are described below. These settings restrict particular uses; they do not mean that all SDK data processing stops.

Remove Ads stops banners and automatic interstitial ads. Eligible players can still choose an optional rewarded ad for a continue or a one-run finish. The reward is granted after the provider confirms it was earned. Removing ads does not erase records already held by a provider.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Advertising privacy choices

At launch, the game checks Google’s User Messaging Platform for the privacy messages and choices that apply to you. Ad requests wait until the provider says they can be made. Required messages and ad availability can vary by region and your choices.

When the provider requires a privacy-options entry point, Ad Privacy Choices appears in the game’s Remove Ads screen. You can use it to review the choices the provider makes available. An adult-area check can protect access for under-18 or unknown-age choices.

You can also manage advertising identifiers and app permissions in device settings. For a privacy question you cannot resolve in the app, email us using the address on this page.

Store purchases and restoration

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play where the product is available. The store displays the local price and handles payment. We do not receive your full payment-card number or store password.

The game uses product IDs, transaction identifiers or purchase tokens, receipt or signed transaction information, and ownership status to check and restore a purchase. A local verified-ownership cache lets the game remember the entitlement. Refunds, revocations or store-account changes can affect ownership when it is checked again.

Purchase services start when a protected store, purchase or restoration flow is opened. This release uses Unity In-App Purchasing with runtime IAP analytics disabled and does not initialize Unity Gaming Services. Apple and Google still process the store information needed for billing, ownership and restoration.

Cars unlocked with earned coins do not invoke store billing. Optional rewarded finishes change appearance for one run; an earned, unused finish reward is kept locally until used.

Read App Store & Privacy, Google’s Privacy Policy and the privacy information for this Unity IAP version.

Age choices and families

The game asks for an age range rather than a date of birth. The selected range stays on your device and determines the protection signals sent to advertising services.

Players under 16 and players who choose not to provide an age range receive child age treatment and can be offered banners only. They are not offered automatic full-screen or rewarded ads. Players aged 16–17 receive teen treatment; eligible players aged 16 or older may use full-screen or rewarded ads, subject to privacy choices and availability.

For under-18 and unknown-age choices, an adult-area check protects the shop, purchases, restoration and links that leave the game. This calculation-based screen lock is an access safeguard; it is not verified parental consent to collect personal information.

Parents or guardians can contact us about privacy, an unexpected purchase or an inappropriate ad. Please share only the information needed to investigate.

When you email us

We receive your email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate the problem or handle a privacy request. Email and store-support providers may also process the correspondence.

Device model, operating-system version, Glidori version and a short explanation are usually enough to begin. Please do not send passwords, full card details or unnecessary personal information.

Storage, providers and your requests

Local progress stays in app storage until replaced or removed. We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Providers apply their own retention practices to records they control; uninstalling the game does not automatically erase those records.

Service providers may process information outside your country. Their privacy notices describe their practices and rights processes. We use supported store and SDK mechanisms, but no device or service can be guaranteed completely secure.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion, or other controls over your information. Contact andrei.rad@syncorm.com. We may ask for details needed to locate the relevant records. For information independently controlled by Apple, Google or GitHub, their own request processes also apply. There is no Glidori player account to close.

These website pages

The code for these pages uses no advertising or analytics scripts, tracking pixels, third-party font downloads, cookies or local-storage features. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, including visits without a GitHub sign-in.

See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. External links open services with their own privacy practices. Email links open your email app; this website has no contact form.

Policy updates

We will revise this page and its date when Glidori’s data practices change. You can always find the current policy at this address or contact us with a question.
