Climb. Land clean. Go higher.

UPSHIFT is an arcade game for Android. Drag to steer a bouncing ball up through 200 levels of turning machines, catch lightning bolts to keep your energy up, and chain clean landings for bigger rewards. A new daily climb every day.

In preparation

The game is not yet available in the stores.

Daily climb

The same course for everyone each day, plus an endless mode once you reach level 10.

Works offline

No account needed. Progress is saved on your device.

Fair ads

Ads are never needed to finish a level. Reward videos are optional; Remove Ads is an optional purchase.
