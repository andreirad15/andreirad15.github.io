How to play

Drag left and right to steer the ball; it bounces on its own. Land in the centre of a ledge for a Perfect and keep the streak going. Avoid spikes and moving machines, and reach the gate at the top of each level.

Energy and stars

Your energy bar drains while you climb; lightning bolts and clean landings refill it. Each level awards up to three stars: clear it, catch every bolt, and finish without a missed landing. After a fall you can choose to watch a reward video to continue from where you were, once per level.

Ads

An occasional ad can appear between runs, never during play, and never in your first session. Reward videos are always optional. If an ad is not available, the game simply continues. Privacy choices, where they apply, are in Settings.

Remove Ads and restore

Remove Ads removes interstitial ads permanently in this game. Optional reward videos remain available. After reinstalling or changing device, open the shop and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same store account. Refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store.

Progress and data

Progress is saved on your device only. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling deletes it. See the privacy policy for details.
