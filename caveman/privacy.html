Local game data

The game does not require an account. Campaign progress, crafted equipment, collected-resource records, the current attempt and sound settings are saved on your device. We do not operate a cloud-save, multiplayer, chat or leaderboard service for this release. There are no in-app purchases.

The core adventure works offline. Local saves are used to restore your game and avoid duplicate rewards. Your operating system or a device backup may copy app data according to its own settings; that is separate from a publisher cloud save. Advertising and privacy-message services, when enabled for eligible players, can process data off the device as described below.

Children and age choice

The intended audience includes children and older players. When advertising is enabled, a neutral prompt asks for a broad age range: Under 13, 13–15, 16 or older or Prefer not to say. The choice is stored on the device to control whether advertising services may start. We do not ask for or store a date of birth, and this choice does not verify identity.

In the current implementation, advertising and advertising-consent SDK requests remain off for users under 16 or whose age is unknown or undisclosed. Players can continue the game without providing an age range. The intended release retains this restriction; native startup behavior still needs final verification.

Parents and guardians can contact us with questions or requests about their child's information. Please avoid sending unnecessary information about a child. If you believe personal information from a child has been processed contrary to this policy, email us so we can investigate and address it with the relevant provider where necessary.

Advertising and privacy messages

For eligible players, the intended release uses Unity LevelPlay/ironSource to mediate advertising, with Google AdMob as a planned ad source and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for regional privacy messages. Active providers and settings must be confirmed for the final build. A bottom banner and an interstitial at the transition after each completed level are planned; unavailable ads do not block play.

When active, advertising services can process IP address and approximate location derived from it, device/app identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, crash logs and performance information. Uses can include delivering and measuring ads, advertising analytics, preventing fraud and diagnosing SDK problems. Device identifiers or ad interactions may be shared among mediation and advertising partners; this can constitute tracking or targeted advertising depending on the provider, settings, choices and applicable law. A lack of a game account does not make advertising data anonymous. See Google's Android disclosure and iOS disclosure.

UMP contacts Google when refreshing the eligible player's privacy status or presenting required choices. It can use technical request information and store consent records so applicable choices can be applied. Privacy-message requests are separate from showing an ad. We do not treat silence as affirmative consent; the intended flow checks the available regional privacy choices before requesting ads. See Google's UMP information.

Read the available messages and use the choices they provide. Where supported, open Pause → Privacy → Ad privacy choices to review or change ad-consent choices. Declining personalized advertising may still permit limited or non-personalized ads under the provider's settings and applicable requirements. On iOS, Apple tracking permission is a separate choice if tracking is requested. Refusing that permission does not prevent the core game. These controls affect future processing and do not automatically delete previous records.

For provider-specific processing, retention, international transfers and privacy-request routes, read Unity's player privacy policy and Google's privacy policy. We do not sell your local saved-game files. Advertising partners' use of identifiers may fall within legal definitions of selling or sharing personal information; use the available privacy choices and contact us about your applicable rights.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer the request, investigate the problem and keep an appropriate record of our response. Please do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal details. Our email provider also processes the correspondence to deliver and store it.

This static website has no analytics code, advertisements, forms, embedded media, custom cookies or remote fonts. The intended host, GitHub Pages, may process request information such as IP address and browser/network details to deliver and secure the site. See the GitHub privacy statement. Following a link to another provider opens a service governed by that provider's own privacy information.

Use, sharing and retention

We use local data to provide the adventure and support information to respond to you. Where personal information is processed under European data-protection law, the applicable basis depends on the activity: providing a service you request, consent where required, legitimate interests in support/security and legal obligations where applicable. Provider purposes and legal bases are explained in their own notices. This policy does not grant blanket consent for advertising or tracking.

Local data stays until it is replaced during play, app storage is cleared or the app is deleted, subject to operating-system backups. Support correspondence is kept while needed to handle the request and related follow-up, and longer only where necessary for legal obligations or a dispute. You may ask us to delete it. We do not set one universal retention period for independent advertising-provider records; their notices and applicable obligations govern those records.

Information may be processed by hosting, email, advertising and technical-service providers outside your country, including outside the EEA. Applicable transfer safeguards depend on the provider and processing route; consult the linked notices or contact us for information about a specific request. Information can also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights or security. We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information, but no storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Choices and data requests

  • Local data: Android app-storage controls can clear the game's data. On iOS, deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading can preserve it. Backups may retain or restore data. Review your device backup settings separately. Clearing data can erase your progress and age choice.
  • Advertising: use the available in-game ad privacy options and device advertising/tracking settings. The current app has no separate change-age button. Contact support for help with device-local controls; we cannot remotely edit your saved age band.
  • Support or provider records: email rovideotuts@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion, or to ask about another privacy right. Say whether the request concerns support correspondence, advertising/privacy-message data or local game data, and include the platform and app version if known.

Where applicable, rights can also include portability, restriction, objection and withdrawal of consent. Withdrawing consent does not change the lawfulness of earlier processing. We may need proportionate information to verify a request or locate provider records; we explain what is needed before asking for sensitive identifiers. Deleting local data or uninstalling does not itself delete advertising-provider records or previous support emails. An email request is not a confirmation that deletion has already occurred.

We respond as required by applicable law, generally within one month for GDPR requests, and explain any permitted extension or reason a request cannot be completed. You may complain to your local supervisory authority, including Romania's ANSPDCP. Read more about individual rights in the EU.

Changes and contact

We update this page when release behavior, providers or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the current revision. Material changes will be communicated as required. Contact Eugen Andrei Rad, Romania, at rovideotuts@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.
