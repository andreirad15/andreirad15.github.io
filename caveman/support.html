Playing

How do I move, jump and attack?

Play in landscape. Hold the left or right movement control, then use the jump button to reach a ledge. You can move and jump together. Use the weapon button to attack, and watch for a creature's warning before it strikes.

Pause to take a break or restart the current trail. The sound control turns game audio on or off.

How do I finish a trail?

Collect all three supplies, resolve the required obstacles and reach the cave. Keys open gates, levers lower bridges and crates can hold down pressure plates. Amber and some side paths are optional discoveries.

How does crafting work?

Collect meat, wood, stone and bones during the adventure. Open crafting to see the resources needed for each weapon. The bone, club and later upgrades use materials earned in the game. Crafting does not take a payment.

What happens after a fall or a hit?

If you run out of health or fall into a pit, you return to a checkpoint. Collected supplies and solved mechanisms are kept for the current attempt. There is no limited-life timer or required ad to continue. Restarting or replaying begins a new attempt on that trail.

Progress and local data

Your campaign progress, crafted equipment, collected-resource records, current attempt and sound preference are stored on your device. No account is required, and there is no publisher cloud-save service. Closing and relaunching the app uses the local save when it is available.

Removing app data can erase your adventure. On Android, use the system app-storage controls to clear the app's storage. On iOS, deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading is different and can preserve it. Your device or operating-system backups may keep or restore a copy. Review those backup controls separately. Replaying a trail is not a full data deletion.

The advertising age-range choice, when requested, also stays on the device. The current game does not offer a separate change-age or erase-all-progress button. Contact support if you need help with the available device controls. Local deletion does not automatically delete earlier support emails or advertising-provider records. See privacy choices and data requests.

Advertising choices

For eligible players, the intended release has a bottom banner and may show an interstitial when leaving the result after each completed level, if an ad is ready. An unavailable ad does not block the next trail. There are no in-app purchases in this release.

When advertising is enabled, the age-range prompt offers Under 13, 13–15, 16 or older and Prefer not to say. The game stores a broad range, not your date of birth. Advertising services stay off for under-16 and undisclosed choices, including an unknown age. Choosing not to provide a range does not prevent you from playing.

For eligible players, read the regional privacy message and make your choices. Open Pause → Privacy → Ad privacy choices to review available ad-consent options when the service provides them. On iOS, an Apple tracking request, if needed and presented, is a separate choice. Refusing tracking must not prevent the core adventure. Read about advertising data and providers.
